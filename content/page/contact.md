## Contact us

Whether you're planning your first trip to Asunción, putting together a longer itinerary
around the country, or figuring out the practical side of moving here, we'd rather talk
it through than have you guess. Send us your dates, your questions, or just what you've
heard and want to check — Anton or Yanina will reply personally, not a call centre.

## How to contact us

- **WhatsApp** — the fastest way to reach us, especially once you're already in the
  country. Use the button on this page or message the number below directly.
- **Email** — [hello@thingstodoinparaguay.com](mailto:hello@thingstodoinparaguay.com) for
  anything that needs detail: a multi-day itinerary, a [residency](/paraguay-residency-service/)
  question, or a group booking.
- **Contact form** — below, for a first message with your dates and what you're after. We
  read every one and reply the same or next business day.
- **Office** — Edificio Skytower, Asunción, Paraguay. Meetings are by arrangement rather
  than drop-in, since we're usually out running tours or transfers.

## What to include

The faster we understand what you need, the faster we can give you a useful answer
rather than a generic one. It helps to tell us:

- Your travel dates (or "not sure yet" is fine too)
- Whether you're after a single [tour](/tours/) or a full itinerary
- How many people, and any mobility or dietary needs we should plan around
- If you're relocating: whether you're at the research stage or ready to start paperwork

We don't publish fixed prices for custom itineraries or relocation packages because they
depend on group size, dates and what you actually want — ask us and we'll quote honestly,
including telling you when something isn't worth paying for.

## Before you write in

A few common questions are already answered on our [FAQ page](/faq/) — worth a quick
check before you wait on a reply. If you're specifically after airport pickup, see the
[airport transfer service](/airport-transfer/) page, which has its own booking details.
If you're checking official travel guidance before you book, the U.S. State Department's
[Paraguay travel advisory](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/paraguay-travel-advisory.html)
is a reasonable starting point regardless of your own nationality.

We read every message ourselves; nothing here goes to an outsourced inbox.
