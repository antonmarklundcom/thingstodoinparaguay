faq:
  - q: Is a custom tour more expensive?
    a: It depends. While private services are premium, we can often save you money by knowing the local prices for hotels and food, avoiding the "tourist surcharges." You pay for exactly what you want, nothing you don't.
  - q: Can you help me find a specific person/relative?
    a: We can help with genealogical trips — visiting colony archives, church records, and cemeteries in places like Filadelfia, Hohenau, or La Colmena — but we're not a professional genealogist or records service. If you already have a name, town, or approximate date, share it in advance so we can plan which archives are worth visiting.
  - q: I want to drive myself. Can you just do the planning?
    a: Yes. If you'd rather self-drive, we can build the route, book accommodation, and brief you on road conditions and checkpoints without providing a driver. Just tell us that's what you want when we start the consultation.
  - q: Can we go to the Chaco?
    a: Yes, the Chaco is one of our most requested add-ons — whether for photography, bird watching, or visiting the [Mennonite colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mennonites_in_Paraguay) around [Filadelfia](/filadelfia-chaco/). It's a long drive from Asunción, so we usually build it as a multi-day leg rather than a single day trip.
  - q: How far in advance should I book?
    a: For simple day trips, 48 hours is fine. For multi-day expeditions involving hotels in the interior, we recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance to secure the best accommodation.
  - q: Is food included?
    a: Not by default — like transport and lodging, meals are built into your custom quote based on what you want. Tell us your preferences and budget and we'll either recommend stops or build them into the plan.
  - q: Do you require a deposit?
    a: Yes, a deposit secures your dates once the itinerary is finalized, with the balance due before or during the trip. Check the exact terms and current amount with us when you book.
  - q: Can I change the plan during the trip?
    a: Within reason, yes. Since this is a private, flexible itinerary, we can adjust stops, add a day, or swap an activity on the ground if time and logistics allow. Bigger changes work best with a little notice so we can rearrange lodging and transport.
