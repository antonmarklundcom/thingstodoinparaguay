  ## The Treasures

  Ñandutí & Ao Po'i (Textiles): Paraguay's most famous export. Ñandutí (Guarani for "Spider Web") is a colorful, intricate lace that can be framed as art or worn. Ao Po'i is the breathable, elegant embroidered cotton used for shirts and dresses. We help you distinguish between machine-made copies and the true hand-woven masterpieces.

  Filigrana (Silver Jewelry): Luque (near Asunción) is famous for filigree—silver spun into threads thinner than hair. We guide you to the trusted jewelers where you can buy earrings, pendants, and bracelets that look like frozen lace.

  Leather & Tereré Kits: The Termo (Thermos) is the national accessory of Paraguay. We help you design a custom, hand-tooled leather termo and Guampa (cup) set, often personalized with your name or logo on the spot. It is the ultimate functional souvenir.

  Pottery & Wood: From the surreal ceramic hens of "La Pote" to the sacred wood carvings of the missions. We find the pieces that fit your suitcase and your home decor.

faq:
  - q: Do artisans accept credit cards?
    a: Many of the established stalls in La Recova do, but the smaller, more authentic artisans often deal only in cash (Guaraníes). We will stop at an ATM beforehand and advise you on how much cash to carry.
  - q: Is it okay to haggle/negotiate?
    a: Yes, but with respect. Unlike some cultures where aggressive haggling is a sport, in Paraguay, it is softer. We will guide you on what is a "fair offer" so you get a good deal without insulting the artist.
  - q: Can I bring these items through customs?
    a: Yes. Processed leather, silver, and textiles are generally safe for entry into the USA, Europe, and Asia. We avoid raw wood or animal products that might cause issues at borders.
  - q: Are these items fragile?
    a: Pottery and Filigree can be delicate. We advise you on how to pack them safely for a flight, or we can help you find bubble wrap and packaging materials during the tour.
  - q: I want to buy in bulk for my shop back home. Can you help?
    a: Yes. If you are looking for export quantities, we can act as a translator to help you establish a business relationship with the artisans.
  - q: Can we customize the leather goods?
    a: Absolutely. Many leather workers can emboss your name or a specific design onto a Thermos or bag within 30-60 minutes while we wait or have lunch.
  - q: Is this tour suitable for children?
    a: Yes, kids often find the colorful markets fascinating, especially watching the artisans at work. However, the markets are not stroller-friendly, so be prepared to walk.
