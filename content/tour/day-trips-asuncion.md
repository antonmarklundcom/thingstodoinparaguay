itinerary:
  - title: "Option A: The Golden Circuit (Circuito de Oro)"
    body: |
      The Classic Cultural Loop. Best for first-time visitors.

      Areguá: Visit the lakeside town famous for colorful ceramics and colonial architecture.

      Itauguá: Watch the weavers make the famous "Ñandutí" (Spiderweb lace).

      San Bernardino: Lunch by the legendary Lake Ypacaraí and a walk through the German-founded summer town.

      Caacupé: Visit the massive Basilica, the spiritual center of the country.
  - title: "Option B: The Franciscan Route (History & Hills)"
    body: |
      Best for photographers and history buffs.

      Yaguarón: Visit the Church of San Buenaventura—the most beautiful wooden church in South America (an artistic masterpiece).

      Paraguarí: Drive through the stunning landscapes of "cerros" (hills).

      Piribebuy: Visit the historic sites of the Triple Alliance War.
  - title: "Option C: Eco-Adventure (Waterfalls & Canopy)"
    body: |
      Best for active travelers.

      Eco-Reserva Mbatoví: (Optional) Zip-lining and hanging bridges in the forest.

      Salto Cristal: (Requires 4×4 upgrade). Hike down to a massive 45m waterfall hidden in the jungle.
