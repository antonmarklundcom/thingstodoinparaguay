  - q: I am a Vegetarian/Vegan. Is there anything for me?
    a: Yes! While Paraguay is famous for beef, the high-end gastronomic scene is very accommodating. Many top restaurants offer gourmet grilled vegetables, pastas, and salads. We will choose a restaurant specifically known for its vegetarian options if you request it.
  - q: Do I need to dress up?
    a: For the restaurants we recommend, yes. The dress code is typically "Smart Casual" or "Elegant." Men should wear long pants and a collared shirt (or nice polo). Women typically dress up for dinner here. It is part of the culture to look good when dining out.
  - q: How much does a high-end dinner cost in Asunción?
    a: You will be pleasantly surprised. A full three-course dinner with wine at a top-tier luxury restaurant in Asunción often costs between $30 and $60 USD per person. It is a fraction of the price of a similar meal in New York or London.
  - q: How do I tip?
    a: A 10% legal gratuity is usually included in the bill. However, for good service, it is customary to leave an additional 5-10% in cash for the waiter. Your guide can help you calculate the appropriate amount so you feel comfortable.
  - q: Can you accommodate food allergies?
    a: Absolutely. This is one of the main benefits of our service. Whether you are gluten-free (Celiac), allergic to nuts, or lactose intolerant, we communicate this strictly to the kitchen staff in Spanish to ensure your safety.

  - q: Is the "Parrilla" (Grill) suitable for children?
    a: Yes, Paraguayans love children and restaurants are very family-friendly. Many larger steakhouses even have dedicated "Kids Zones" with nannies. If you are traveling with family, let us know, and we will select a venue that keeps the kids entertained while you enjoy your wine.
