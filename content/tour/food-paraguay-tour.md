Our food Paraguay tour uncovers the unique flavors of the "Heart of South America." From the cheesy crunch of hot Chipa to the legendary street-side Lomito, we take you to the places locals love, ensuring authentic flavors and total peace of mind.

[Book My Tasting Experience]

Paraguayan cuisine is delicious, but it can be intimidating for a newcomer. Menus are often in Guaraní, street food hygiene standards vary wildly, and finding the best version of a dish (rather than just a greasy one) takes local insider knowledge. Don't gamble with your stomach. We curate a route of sanitary, high-quality, and authentic establishments so you can explore the flavors without the worry.

We start as the locals do. You will taste the Chipa (a cheesy corn/manioc bagel) fresh out of the tatakua (brick oven) and the Mbeju, a starch pancake that is crispy on the outside and gooey on the inside. We'll pair it with a sweet Cocido Quemado (burnt mate tea) to wake up your palate.

Did you know the "National Soup" of Paraguay is actually a solid cake? You will try the authentic Sopa Paraguaya (cornbread with onions and cheese) and Chipa Guazú (corn soufflé). If the weather permits, we'll dive into Vorí Vorí, a comforting chicken soup with corn dumplings that was recently voted the best soup in the world.
