  ## High Growth. Low Taxes. Hidden Risks.

  Paraguay is currently a magnet for regional capital. The construction boom in Asunción and the agricultural strength of the interior are undeniable.

  But the market is opaque. There is no centralized "MLS" (Multiple Listing Service). Prices for the same hectare of land can vary by 50% depending on who is asking. And—most critically—verifying Land Titles is a complex legal hurdle. Without a local guide, it is easy to overpay or buy property with legal complications.
