itinerary:
  - title: The Drive Through the Countryside
    body: The journey itself is an experience. We drive down Route 1, passing through the quiet towns of the interior, seeing the rolling hills, cattle ranches, and the changing soil color from brown to deep crimson red.
  - title: La Santísima Trinidad del Paraná
    body: The jewel of the crown. We walk through the largest and best-preserved mission. You will stand in the massive central plaza, explore the intricate friezes of angels playing Guaraní instruments carved into stone, and visit the ancient crypt. The scale of the church—even in ruins—is breathtaking.
  - title: Jesús de Tavarangue
    body: A short drive away lies the unfinished mission. Known for its unique Moorish-style arches (Trefoil arches), this site feels different—more open, more architectural. It was never finished before the Jesuits were expelled in 1767, leaving it as a ghostly snapshot of a construction site frozen in time.
