faq:
  - q: Is it cheaper to buy electronics here than in the USA?
    a: In many cases, yes, or very comparable. Paraguay has a low tax regime on imported electronics. However, the real advantage is availability and the lack of sales tax compared to some US states/European VAT.
  - q: Can I pay with US Dollars?
    a: Yes, most electronics stores love US Dollars. In fact, you often get a better price paying in cash USD than with a credit card. We will advise you on when to use cash vs. card.
  - q: Will my electronics work in my home country?
    a: We ensure you buy "Global Version" unlocked devices. We check the voltage (Paraguay is 220V) and plug types to ensure compatibility or help you buy the correct adapters.
  - q: Do you take commission from the stores?
    a: Our loyalty is to you, the client. While some guides take commissions to steer you to expensive shops, we prioritize getting you the best deal to ensure you leave a 5-star review. We work for you.
  - q: Is this a walking tour?
    a: No. Shopping in Asunción requires driving between districts (e.g., moving from the Old Center to the new Corporate Axis). We use a car to maximize your time and comfort.
  - q: Can we visit the "Mercado 4"?
    a: Mercado 4 is an intense, chaotic, and fascinating experience (like the movie 7 Cajas). If you are adventurous, yes, we can guide you there safely. However, for high-end electronics or luxury fashion, we stick to the safer, formal commercial districts. See our [Asunción guide](/what-to-do-in-asuncion/) for other things worth building into the same day.
  - q: What is the best day to shop?
    a: Weekdays (Monday-Friday) are best to avoid crowds. However, malls are open and lively on weekends. Avoid Sunday mornings for downtown/street shops as many will be closed.
  - q: Do you help with shipping?
    a: If you buy large items (like furniture or art), we can connect you with reliable international courier services (DHL/FedEx) to arrange shipping home, though this is a separate cost.
