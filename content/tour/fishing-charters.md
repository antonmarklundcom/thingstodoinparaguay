  ## Fishing Charters on a River of Giants

  Paraguay is a bucket-list destination for serious anglers. The massive river systems here are teeming with life. We aren't talking about small panfish; we are talking about the Surubí (a catfish that can weigh 50kg+) and the Dorado (famous for its acrobatic jumps and steel-trap jaws).

itinerary:
  - title: The Golden Dorado (El Tigre)
    body: The prize trophy. Known for its brilliant gold scales and ferocious temper. Pound for pound, it is one of the hardest fighting fish in the world. Catching one is a badge of honor.
  - title: The Surubí (The Giant)
    body: The king of the bottom. These spotted catfish grow to massive sizes. Hooking one feels like you are pulling up a sunken car. They are powerful, stubborn, and distinctively beautiful with their leopard-print skin.
  - title: Pacú & Boga (The Table Fare)
    body: Pound for pound, the Pacú (river pig) offers an incredible fight and is delicious to eat. We often stop on a sandy island to cook your fresh catch over an open fire for lunch.
