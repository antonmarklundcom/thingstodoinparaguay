  ## The Journey

  The Golden Hour Rooftop: We begin the evening by elevating your perspective. We will pick you up and whisk you away to one of the city's premier rooftops in the corporate center or the historic bay area. There is nothing quite like watching the transition of the Asunción skyline while sipping a perfectly chilled Gin & Tonic or a glass of South American wine. It is the perfect moment to breathe, relax, and let the humid air turn into a cool evening breeze.

  The Hunt for the Speakeasy: Once the sun is down, the adventure truly begins. Asunción has embraced the global trend of "hidden bars," but here they have a unique Latin flair. We will guide you through the unassuming doors—perhaps a refrigerator door in a burger joint or a bookshelf in an old house—that lead to intimate, low-lit lounges. This is where we sit down to appreciate the art of mixology, tasting cocktails that infuse local herbs like cedrón or burrito into modern classics.

  The Taste of Tradition: No night in Paraguay is complete without understanding Caña. We will make a stop dedicated to our national spirit, a premium sugar cane rum aged in wood. Unlike the industrial rum you might know, Paraguayan Caña is complex and rich. Your guide will explain the history behind the drink while you enjoy a "Paraguayito" (our version of the Caipirinha) or a neat pour of an aged reserve.

  - q: Is there a strict dress code?
    a: Yes, Asunción's nightlife is fashion-forward. We recommend a "Smart Casual" look. For men, long trousers and closed shoes are usually required for entry into clubs and upscale bars (no flip-flops or athletic shorts). For women, comfortable but chic attire works best. We will advise you on the specific vibe for your scheduled night.
  - q: How much should I budget for drinks?
    a: Asunción offers incredible value compared to the US or Europe. A high-end signature cocktail in a top speakeasy typically costs between $6 and $10 USD. Local beers are significantly cheaper. Since you pay the venues directly, you have total control over your spending.
  - q: Do bars accept credit cards or do I need cash?
    a: 95% of the venues we visit accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). However, we recommend carrying a small amount of Guarani (local cash) for tips or smaller, authentic "dive bar" stops that might not have a card terminal.
  - q: I don't drink alcohol. Is this tour still worth it?
    a: Absolutely. The "Bars Asuncion" tour is about the atmosphere, the architecture, and the social experience. Asunción's mixologists are experts at creating "Mocktails" (alcohol-free cocktails) using fresh tropical fruits and herbs. You can enjoy the rooftop views and the speakeasy vibe without the hangover.
  - q: Is tipping customary in Paraguayan bars?
    a: In Paraguay, a 10% service charge is often included in the bill at sit-down venues. However, if the service is exceptional, leaving a small extra tip is appreciated but not mandatory. We can guide you on the etiquette at each location so you never feel awkward.

  - q: Is this suitable for solo travelers?

  - q: Do I need to bring my Passport?
    a: We recommend bringing a photo of your passport on your phone and a physical form of ID (like a driver's license). Some nightclubs and secure venues require an ID check at the door for age verification. We will handle the communication with the doormen for you.
